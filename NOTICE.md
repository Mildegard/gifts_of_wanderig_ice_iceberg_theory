This repository mixes material with different owners. This file sets out what is whose, so that reuse is straightforward and nobody has to guess.

## Source material — © Olga Makarova (Mildegard)

**Files:** `source/comic_description.md`, `source/pages_866_to_904.md`, `source/pages_905_to_923.md`, `source/image_urls.md`

**What it is:** the webcomic *Gifts of Wandering Ice*, its page images (linked and not reproduced as files), and the author's per-page commentary blocks, quoted verbatim.

**Status:** reproduced **with the author's permission.** The repository was created at her suggestion, and she is the one hosting it. Olga keeps all rights to the comic, the images, and her commentary.

**If you want to reuse the source material:** ask her. Do not treat its presence here as a licence.

**Note on the quoted commentary:** the author notes are personal in places — they discuss the author's health, mood, and life circumstances. They are quoted here because the experiment's central finding concerns *how those notes differ from the pages they accompany*. **Quoting them for that purpose is not the same as treating them as available for any use.** If you are writing about this repo and want to quote the personal passages, do it with care and context, not as a hook.

## Third-party reader comments — © their individual authors

**Where:** the Disqus comment threads on the comic, ~393 comments across the pages in range.

**Status:** **summarised, not reproduced.** Individual comments, usernames, and full threads do **not** appear in this repository. Findings drawn from them are stated as findings; the comments themselves are not republished.

**Why:** the commenters commented on a comic, not into a research study. Republishing their words in an analytic frame they did not agree to is a different act from reading them.

**What this costs:** reproducibility. The largest-n human layer in the experiment is the one a reader of this repo cannot check. This is recorded as a real limitation in `CAVEATS.md`, not waved away.

**If you are one of those commenters** and you object to how your observation has been generalised here, that is a legitimate objection. Raise it and it will be addressed.

**Where:** `platform_replies/`

**Status:** the posts quoted are public posts made in public threads on public platforms. ## A note on the whole repository

**This repository is an experiment's lab notebook, not a paper.** It states things that are provisional, contradictory, and possibly wrong, and it keeps track of which are which. If you cite it, **cite the caveats with it.** A claim from `FINDINGS.md` quoted without the corresponding entry in `CAVEATS.md` is not a claim this repository is making.
